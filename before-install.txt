Welcome to Hydra Manga TL 0.8.0

Hydra Manga TL translates Japanese and Chinese manga pages into English using local OCR and translation models.

Release track:
- v0.8.0 is finished and currently under polishing.
- v0.7.0 introduced the HydraMangaAi milestone and remains the legacy bridge layer.

Important:
- Windows 10 or Windows 11 64-bit is required.
- Several gigabytes of free disk space are recommended.
- An internet connection is required during the first translation so the AI models can be downloaded.
- Original manga images are never modified.
- Automatic translations should be reviewed before publication.
