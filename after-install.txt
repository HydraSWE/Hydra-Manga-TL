Hydra Manga TL v0.8.0 has been installed successfully.

After installation:
1. Launch Hydra Manga TL from the Start Menu or Desktop.
2. Import manga images or a folder.
3. Select Translate All Pending.
4. Review and correct the translated pages.
5. Export the completed PNG images.

Release notes:
- v0.8.0 is the finished pipeline checkpoint and is currently under polishing.
- v0.7.0 was the HydraMangaAi milestone and remains available as the legacy bridge layer.

The first translation may take longer while OCR and translation models download.

Projects and logs are stored under:
